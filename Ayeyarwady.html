Top Attraction

1. Chaung Tha Beach

History & Background

Located on the Bay of Bengal coast, Chaung Tha is one of the most popular and accessible beach destinations in Myanmar. Known for its lively local atmosphere, fresh seafood, and nearby attractions like Pokala Island and The White Sand Island.

Best Time to Visit November to April (Ideal for swimming, boat trips, and water sports)
Location Pathein Township, Ayeyarwady Region (5 hours drive from Yangon)
Estimated Cost Free Beach Access / Island Boat Trip: ~10,000 - 20,000 MMK

2. Ngwe Saung Beach

History & Background

Opened in 2000, Ngwe Saung ('Silver Beach') stretches over 15 kilometers of pristine shoreline. Famous for its clear blue waters, quiet sandy beaches, and Lovers' Island (Lover's Island), which can be reached on foot during low tide.

Best Time to Visit 7:00 AM - 10:00 AM / 4:00 PM - 6:30 PM (Great for relaxing and sunset views)
Location 48 km west of Pathein, Ayeyarwady Region
Estimated Cost Free Beach Access / Motorbike Rental: ~15,000 MMK per day

3. Goyangyi Island (Gaw Yan Gyi)

History & Background

A hidden gem in southern Ayeyarwady, Goyangyi features stunning rock formations, crystal-clear turquoise waters, and untouched natural beaches. It offers an adventurous coastal escape with scenic viewpoints atop coastal cliffs.

Best Time to Visit November to March (Best sea conditions for camping and island trekking)
Location Ngapudaw Township, Southwestern Ayeyarwady Region
Estimated Cost Free Access / Local Boat Crossing: ~5,000 - 10,000 MMK

4. Pathein City & Parasol Workshops

History & Background

Pathein, the capital of Ayeyarwady Region, is famous across Myanmar for its traditional hand-painted cotton and silk parasols ('Pathein Hti'). Visiting local family-run workshops offers a glimpse into this centuries-old Myanmar craft.

Best Time to Visit 9:00 AM - 5:00 PM (Watch artisans hand-paint traditional umbrellas)
Location Downtown Pathein, Ayeyarwady Region
Estimated Cost Free Workshop Entry / Handcrafted Parasols: ~5,000 - 25,000 MMK

5. Meinmahla Kyun Wildlife Sanctuary

History & Background

A crucial Ramsar wetland site located in the Ayeyarwady Delta, this mangrove island sanctuary protects endangered saltwater crocodiles, migratory waterbirds, and Irrawaddy dolphins in their natural coastal habitat.

Best Time to Visit November to February (Cool season for migratory bird watching and river safaris)
Location Bogale Township, Outer Delta, Ayeyarwady Region
Estimated Cost Sanctuary Entry & Boat Charter: ~50,000 - 100,000 MMK (group sharing)
