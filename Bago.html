Top Attraction

1. Shwemawdaw Pagoda

History & Background

Known as the 'Great Golden God Pagoda', Shwemawdaw was built by the Mon people over 1,000 years ago. Standing at 114 meters, it is the tallest pagoda in Myanmar, even taller than Shwedagon Pagoda, and enshrines sacred hair relics of Gautama Buddha.

Best Time to Visit 6:00 AM - 8:00 AM / 4:30 PM - 7:00 PM (Best for cool weather and photography)
Location Pagan Road, Bago Township, Bago Region
Estimated Cost Bago Zone Fee: ~$10 USD (covers multiple sites) / Free for locals

2. Shwethalyaung Reclining Buddha

History & Background

Built in 994 AD by Mon King Migadepa II, this magnificent 55-meter-long reclining Buddha image was lost to the jungle after Bago was destroyed in 1757. It was rediscovered in 1881 during British railway construction and restored to its full glory.

Best Time to Visit 8:00 AM - 5:00 PM (Sheltered pavilion, comfortable to visit anytime)
Location West of Bago Downtown, Bago Region
Estimated Cost Included in Bago Zone Fee / Souvenirs: ~5,000 - 15,000 MMK

3. Kanbawzathadi Palace

History & Background

Originally constructed in 1556 by King Bayinnaung, founder of the Hanthawaddy Empire (the second Myanmar Empire), this grand royal palace had 76 apartments and halls. The current golden structure is a faithful reconstruction built in the 1990s based on original excavation maps.

Best Time to Visit 9:00 AM - 4:30 PM (Explore the museum interior and teak throne rooms)
Location Palace Road, Bago Township, Bago Region
Estimated Cost Included in Bago Zone Ticket / Camera Fee: ~1,000 MMK

4. Kyaikpun Pagoda

History & Background

Built by King Migadi in the 7th century and restored by King Dhammazedi in 1476, Kyaikpun is famous for its four 27-meter-tall Buddha statues seated back-to-back facing the four cardinal directions (Kakusandha, Konagamana, Kassapa, and Gautama).

Best Time to Visit 8:00 AM - 11:00 AM / 3:30 PM - 5:30 PM (Great for outdoor photography)
Location 4 km southwest of Bago, on the Yangon-Bago highway
Estimated Cost Included in Bago Zone Fee / Tuk-tuk from Bago center: ~4,000 MMK

5. Sri Ksetra Pyu Ancient City (Pyay)

History & Background

Dating back from the 5th to the 9th century AD, Sri Ksetra was the largest capital of the ancient Pyu Kingdom. Recognized as a UNESCO World Heritage Site, it features ancient cylindrical brick stupas like Bawbawgyi and ancient burial mounds.

Best Time to Visit 8:00 AM - 4:00 PM (Rent a bicycle or motorbike to tour the ruins)
Location Hmawza Village, Pyay Township, Western Bago Region
Estimated Cost Site Entrance: ~5,000 MMK / Bicycle Rental: ~3,000 MMK
