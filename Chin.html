Top Attraction

1. Mount Victoria (Nat Ma Taung / Khonuamthung)

History & Background

Standing at 3,053 meters, Mount Victoria is the highest peak in Chin State and the third highest in Myanmar. Located inside Nat Ma Taung National Park, an ASEAN Heritage Park, it is renowned for its rare white white-browed nuthatch birds, native rhododendron forests, and sea-of-clouds vistas.

Best Time to Visit November to February (Best for blooming red/white rhododendrons and clear mountain views)
Location Kanpetlet Township, Southern Chin State
Estimated Cost Park Entry: Free / Motorbike/4WD Trek transport: ~30,000 - 50,000 MMK

2. Rih Lake (Rih Li)

History & Background

A natural heart-shaped lake located right on the Myanmar-India border. According to ancient Chin folklore and indigenous spiritual beliefs, Rih Lake is the gateway through which all departed spirits must pass on their journey to the afterlife.

Best Time to Visit October to May (Serene atmosphere, crystal-clear water reflections)
Location Falam Township, Northern Chin State (near Zokhawthar border)
Estimated Cost Free Access / Local Boat Tour around Lake: ~10,000 MMK

3. Mindat & Kanpetlet Traditional Villages

History & Background

Mindat and Kanpetlet are cultural centers home to the Dai, Muun, and Ya ethnic tribes. These towns are famous for their ancient traditions, unique musical flute playing using the nose, and elder women with intricate full-face facial tattoos.

Best Time to Visit 8:00 AM - 4:00 PM (Great for trekking between remote mountain villages)
Location Mindat & Kanpetlet Townships, Southern Chin State
Estimated Cost Trekking Guide Fee: ~25,000 - 40,000 MMK per day

4. Hakha City & Timit Valley

History & Background

Hakha is the capital of Chin State, built on a steep mountain slope at an elevation of 1,867 meters. Surrounded by pine forests and scenic valleys like Timit, it offers stunning mountain scenery and colorful Lai Chin culture.

Best Time to Visit 7:00 AM - 5:00 PM (Visit Hakha Viewpoint Peak for morning sea of clouds)
Location Hakha Township, Northern Chin State
Estimated Cost Free Exploration / Local Motorbike Taxi: ~5,000 MMK

5. Bungtla Waterfall (Bungtla Vawnh)

History & Background

Bungtla Waterfall is one of the longest and most impressive multi-tiered cascades in Chin State, tumbling down nine distinct stone steps amidst deep virgin mountain jungle. It is an adventurous destination for nature lovers and hikers.

Best Time to Visit October to January (Flowing water is clear and jungle trails are safe)
Location Matupi Township, Central-Southern Chin State
Estimated Cost Free Entry / Local Trail Guide: ~15,000 - 20,000 MMK
