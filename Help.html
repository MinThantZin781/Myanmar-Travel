Travel Myanmar Help Center

Need help? Search for topics or ask your questions directly below.

1. Trip Cancellation & Amendments

How do I cancel my booking?

Log in to your Account, go to the "My Bookings" section, select the trip you wish to cancel, and click "Cancel Booking".

Will I get a refund if I cancel my trip?

A 100% refund will be provided for cancellations made at least 7 days prior to departure. A 50% refund applies for cancellations made 3 to 6 days prior, and no refund is provided for cancellations made within 48 hours.

Can I change my travel dates or passenger name?

Date or passenger name changes are free of charge up to 3 days before departure. Additional service fees may apply for changes requested closer to the travel date.

What happens if a trip is cancelled due to weather or emergency?

If the company cancels a trip, a full refund (100%) will be issued or you may reschedule to another suitable date free of charge.

2. Payments & Invoices

Which payment methods are supported?

We accept KBZPay, WavePay, CBPay, AYA Pay, as well as direct payments via Visa and Mastercard.

How do I confirm if my payment was successful?

Once the payment is successful, a Booking Confirmation Email and SMS will be sent to you automatically.

What should I do if my payment fails?

If funds were deducted from your account but you did not receive confirmation, please contact Customer Support with your Transaction ID.

Can I request an Official Invoice/Receipt?

Yes, you can download a PDF invoice directly from the "Download Invoice" button on the "My Bookings" page.

3. Bookings & Services

What services are included in the trip?

Inclusions depend on the package type, but generally include hotels, transportation, tour guides, and breakfast. Please check specific package details for customized itineraries.

Can I make individual or group bookings?

Yes. For group travel (more than 10 people), you can contact the Support Desk to request special group discounts.

Are there separate ticket rates for children?

Children under 2 years old travel for free, while children aged 2 to 12 get a 50% discount.

4. Visa, Passport & Travel Rules

Do you assist foreign travelers with e-Visa applications?

Yes, we provide free assistance with e-Visa applications for foreign travelers who book trips with us.

What documents do I need to bring during travel?

You must bring your original National Registration Card (NRC) or original Passport.

5. Account Management

What should I do if I forget my password?

Click on "Forgot Password" on the Login page, and you can reset your password via the verification link sent to your email.

How do I update my personal information in my account?

Go to Profile Settings to update your phone number, email address, and mailing address anytime.
