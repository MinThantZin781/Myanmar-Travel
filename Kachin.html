Top Attraction

1. Putao Valley & Mount Hkakabo Razi

History & Background

Located in the far north of Myanmar, Putao is a secluded Himalayan valley surrounded by snow-capped mountains year-round. It serves as the gateway to Mount Hkakabo Razi (5,881 meters), Southeast Asia's highest peak, and offers world-class alpine trekking, white-water rafting, and endemic wildlife.

Best Time to Visit October to April (Clear winter skies, crisp snow-peak views, and optimal trekking conditions)
Location Putao District, Far Northern Kachin State
Estimated Cost Flight to Putao + Guided Trekking Package: ~$300 - $800 USD

2. Indawgyi Lake & Shwe Myitzu Pagoda

History & Background

Indawgyi Lake is the largest natural inland lake in Myanmar and a UNESCO Biosphere Reserve. It is famous for hosting thousands of migratory birds and the iconic Shwe Myitzu Pagoda, a floating golden shrine that appears to sit directly on the lake's surface.

Best Time to Visit December to March (Peak migratory bird-watching season and clear kayaking weather)
Location Mohnyin Township, Southwestern Kachin State
Estimated Cost Free Lake Access / Kayak Rental or Boat Trip: ~15,000 - 30,000 MMK

3. Myitsone Confluence (Ayeyarwady River Origin)

History & Background

The symbolic birthplace of Myanmar's mother river, the Ayeyarwady. Myitsone is the scenic confluence where the Mali and N'Mai rivers meet in a rush of clear turquoise waters against a backdrop of lush green mountain ridges.

Best Time to Visit 8:00 AM - 5:00 PM (Ideal day trip from Myitkyina; best water clarity in dry season)
Location 45 km north of Myitkyina, Kachin State
Estimated Cost Free Access / Local Motorbike or Car Taxi from Myitkyina: ~20,000 - 40,000 MMK

4. Myitkyina City & Manau Park

History & Background

Myitkyina is the vibrant capital city of Kachin State situated along the Ayeyarwady River. The town center is famous for the Manau Park, featuring towering, colorfully carved ceremonial Manau poles used during traditional Kachin New Year celebrations.

Best Time to Visit January (Kachin State Day & traditional Manau dance festival) or October to March
Location Downtown Myitkyina, Kachin State
Estimated Cost Free Park Entry / Local Market Shopping & Food: ~5,000 - 15,000 MMK

5. Bhamo (Bamaw Old River Town)

History & Background

A historic border trading town set along the upper stretches of the Ayeyarwady River. Bhamo has centuries of history as a key trade hub between Myanmar and China, known for its scenic river gorges, wooden monasteries, and diverse ethnic markets.

Best Time to Visit November to February (Best weather for river cruise journeys between Mandalay and Bhamo)
Location Bhamo District, Southern Kachin State
Estimated Cost Free City Exploration / Riverboat Passenger Ticket: ~15,000 - 35,000 MMK
