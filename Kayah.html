Top Attraction

1. Taung Kwe Pagoda (Thiri Mingalar Taung Kwe)

History & Background

Perched dramatically atop a split limestone crag in Loikaw, Taung Kwe Pagoda is the most famous landmark in Kayah State. Built piece by piece across separate rock pinnacles, it offers breathtaking 360-degree views of Loikaw town and surrounding blue mountains.

Best Time to Visit 5:00 PM - 7:00 PM (Stunning panoramic sunset and illuminated nighttime view)
Location Downtown Loikaw, Kayah State
Estimated Cost Free Entry / Elevator access available

2. Pan Pet Village (Kayan Ethnic Village)

History & Background

Home to the Kayan Lahwi ethnic community, famously known for their women who wear traditional brass neck coils. Pan Pet is a pioneer in community-based tourism, offering visitors an authentic cultural experience and local handicrafts.

Best Time to Visit 9:00 AM - 3:00 PM (Great for community trekking and cultural interaction)
Location Demoso Township, Kayah State (approx. 1 hour drive from Loikaw)
Estimated Cost Community Tour Guide Fee: ~15,000 - 25,000 MMK

3. Htee Pree Blue Lake (7 Stage Lake / Htee Mue Htee)

History & Background

A pristine natural pond set amidst farmland and mountains, Htee Pree is famous for its vibrant turquoise-blue water caused by natural mineral deposits. It is considered a sacred water site by local villagers.

Best Time to Visit 8:30 AM - 4:00 PM (Best in bright daylight when the blue color pops)
Location Demoso Township, Kayah State
Estimated Cost Free Entry / Local snacks and drinks nearby: ~3,000 MMK

4. Kyat Cave (Yway Ngu / Ghost Cave)

History & Background

An impressive 2.2 km long natural limestone cave containing mysterious ancient wooden coffins dating back hundreds of years. Local folklore connects the cave to spirits and ancient cave-dwelling tribes.

Best Time to Visit 9:00 AM - 3:30 PM (Torch/flashlight and local guide required inside)
Location 15 km east of Loikaw, Kayah State
Estimated Cost Local Guide & Flashlight Rental: ~5,000 - 10,000 MMK

5. Lawpita Waterfalls & Hser Mu Lake

History & Background

Lawpita is historical as the site of Myanmar's first major hydroelectric power project, fed by Bilu Creek. The surrounding scenic landscape features lush valleys, tranquil lakes, and cascading natural waterfalls.

Best Time to Visit 10:00 AM - 4:00 PM (Great for scenic day drives around Loikaw valley)
Location Lawpita Area, 20 km south of Loikaw, Kayah State
Estimated Cost Free Access / Motorbike or Car Taxi Rental from Loikaw
