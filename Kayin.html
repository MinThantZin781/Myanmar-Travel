Top Attraction

1. Mount Zwegabin & Lumbini Garden

History & Background

Rising 723 meters above the surrounding plains, Mount Zwegabin is the iconic landmark of Kayin State. At its base lies the Lumbini Garden, featuring over 1,000 identical Buddha statues. The peak houses a sacred monastery and offers sweeping views of Hpa-An.

Best Time to Visit 5:00 AM - 10:00 AM (Start early to beat the heat during the 2-hour hike)
Location Hpa-An Township, Kayin State
Estimated Cost Mountain Hiking Fee: ~4,000 MMK / Free Garden Entry

2. Saddan Cave (Sadan Cave)

History & Background

A massive, cathedral-like limestone cave filled with Buddha statues, pagodas, and stalactites. Walking through the cave leads to a hidden lake on the other side, where local wooden boats take visitors back through a flooded tunnel beneath the mountain.

Best Time to Visit 8:00 AM - 4:00 PM (Best during dry season when the cave walk is fully open)
Location 30 km southern outskirts of Hpa-An, Kayin State
Estimated Cost Cave Entry: ~1,000 MMK / Boat Ride Back: ~5,000 - 10,000 MMK

3. Kyaut Ka Latt Pagoda

History & Background

Famous for its gravity-defying architecture, this pagoda sits atop a narrow, sheer limestone rock formation in the middle of an artificial lake. It provides a stunning backdrop with Mount Zwegabin towering in the distance.

Best Time to Visit 7:00 AM - 10:00 AM / 4:00 PM - 6:00 PM (Great for reflection photography on the lake)
Location South of Hpa-An Downtown, Kayin State
Estimated Cost Free Entry / Donations accepted

4. Kawgun Cave

History & Background

Dating back to the 7th century AD during the Mon kingdom era, this natural rock cave contains thousands of tiny clay Buddha images and carvings embedded directly into the steep limestone wall surfaces.

Best Time to Visit 8:00 AM - 5:00 PM (Easy to access and explore year-round)
Location Near Kawgun Village, 13 km from Hpa-An, Kayin State
Estimated Cost Entrance Fee: ~3,000 MMK / Free for locals

5. Bat Cave (Linno Cave) & Ya The Pyan Cave

History & Background

Bat Cave is famous for the breathtaking sunset spectacle where millions of bats stream out of the cave entrance in a continuous line over the Thanlwin River. Nearby Ya The Pyan Cave features ancient hermit legends and scenic viewpoints.

Best Time to Visit 5:30 PM - 6:30 PM (Essential timing for the evening bat flight)
Location Thanlwin Riverbank, near Hpa-An, Kayin State
Estimated Cost Free Entry / Local viewpoint seat fee: ~1,000 MMK
