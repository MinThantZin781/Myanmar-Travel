Top Attraction

1. Myathalun Pagoda

History & Background

Situated on Nagabatt Mountain overlooking the Ayeyarwady River, Myathalun Pagoda was built by a wealthy man named Bedawza in 535 BC. It is famous for housing a sacred bedstead relic (Thalun) made of pure emerald, which Buddha used during his lifetime.

Best Time to Visit 5:00 PM - 7:00 PM (Offers incredible river views and sunset breezes)
Location Riverfront, Magway City, Magway Region
Estimated Cost Free Entry / Tuk-tuk from Magway Center: ~3,000 MMK

2. Minbu Mud Volcano (Naga Pwet Taung)

History & Background

A natural geological wonder, Naga Pwet Taung ('Dragon Dragon Bubbling Mountain') features active mud volcanoes bubbling from subterranean natural gas pressure. Local legend attributes the bubbling activity to a sacred subterranean dragon king.

Best Time to Visit 8:00 AM - 11:00 AM / 4:00 PM - 6:00 PM (Cooler weather for walking)
Location Minbu Township, across the river from Magway City
Estimated Cost Free Entry / Taxi from Magway: ~15,000 - 20,000 MMK

3. Mann Shwesettaw Pagoda

History & Background

Located on the banks of the Mann Creek, this site features two sacred Buddha footprints (Settawya): one on the hill (Ahtet Settawya) and one in the river bed (Akyaw Settawya). The famous annual pilgrimage festival runs continuously for over two months.

Best Time to Visit February to April (During the annual Shwesettaw Festival season)
Location Minbu Township, Magway Region (50 km from Magway)
Estimated Cost Free Entry / Bamboo cabin rental near creek: ~10,000 - 25,000 MMK

4. Thiho Shin Pagoda (Pakokku)

History & Background

Built in 1093 AD by King Alaungsithu of Bagan, the pagoda houses a sacred Buddha image presented by the King of Ceylon (Sri Lanka), giving it the name 'Thiho Shin' (Lord from Ceylon). It is Pakokku's most revered spiritual landmark.

Best Time to Visit 7:00 AM - 6:00 PM (Especially lively during May/June festival time)
Location Pakokku Township, Northern Magway Region
Estimated Cost Free Entry / Donations accepted

5. Beikthano Pyu Ancient City

History & Background

Dating back to between the 2nd century BC and 9th century AD, Beikthano ('Vishnu City') is the oldest Pyu city site in Myanmar. Recognized as a UNESCO World Heritage Site, it contains ancient brick city walls, burial halls, and early stupa foundations.

Best Time to Visit 8:30 AM - 4:00 PM (Morning is ideal for walking around archaeological ruins)
Location Taungdwingyi Township, Eastern Magway Region
Estimated Cost Site Entry Fee: ~5,000 MMK for foreign visitors
