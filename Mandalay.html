Top Attraction

1. U Bein Bridge

History & Background

Built around 1850 by the mayor U Bein, this 1.2-kilometer bridge is believed to be the oldest and longest teakwood bridge in the world. It was constructed using reclaimed teak timber from the former royal palace in Ava (Inwa).

Best Time to Visit 5:00 PM - 6:30 PM (Famous worldwide for its stunning sunset views)
Location Taungthaman Lake, Amarapura Township, Mandalay
Estimated Cost Free Entry / Boat Ride for Sunset: ~15,000 - 25,000 MMK

2. Mandalay Hill

History & Background

Standing at 240 meters, Mandalay Hill is the origin of the city's name. According to legend, Gautama Buddha visited the hill and prophesied that a great city would be founded at its foot on the 2400th anniversary of his teachings.

Best Time to Visit 4:30 PM - 6:30 PM (Ideal for panoramic views of the city and sunset)
Location Northeast of Mandalay Downtown, Aungmyethazan Township
Estimated Cost Camera Fee: 1,000 MMK / Elevator/Escalator Access included

3. Mahamuni Buddha Temple

History & Background

Houses one of Myanmar's most revered Buddha statues, brought from Rakhine in 1784 by Crown Prince Thado Minsaw. Over centuries, male devotees have applied gold leaf to the image, creating a thick, bumpy layer of pure gold several inches thick.

Best Time to Visit 4:00 AM (For daily face-washing ceremony) or 8:00 AM - 5:00 PM
Location Maha Nwe Zin Ward, Chanmyathazi Township, Mandalay
Estimated Cost Mandalay Zone Ticket: ~$10 USD / Gold Leaf Packet: ~5,000 MMK

4. Kuthodaw Pagoda (The World's Largest Book)

History & Background

Built by King Mindon in 1857, this pagoda is world-famous for housing 729 white marble pavilions, each containing a stone slab inscribed on both sides with Theravada Buddhist scriptures (Tripitaka).

Best Time to Visit 8:00 AM - 11:00 AM / 3:30 PM - 5:30 PM (Great for photography)
Location At the base of Mandalay Hill, Aungmyethazan Township
Estimated Cost Included in Mandalay Archaeological Zone Ticket

5. Mandalay Royal Palace

History & Background

Constructed between 1857 and 1859 by King Mindon as part of the new royal capital, this walled palace was the last royal residence of the Burmese monarchy (King Mindon and King Thibaw).

Best Time to Visit 9:00 AM - 4:30 PM (Passport or ID required at entry gate)
Location Between 12th and 26th Streets, Downtown Mandalay
Estimated Cost Mandalay Archaeological Zone Fee: ~10,000 MMK / ~$10 USD
