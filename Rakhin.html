Top Attraction

1. Mrauk-U Ancient Kingdom

History & Background

Founded in 1430, Mrauk-U was the powerful capital of the Arakanese Kingdom for over 350 years. Famous for its unique fortress-like stone temples such as Shitte-thaung and Kothaung, Mrauk-U offers a mesmerizing historical landscape often shrouded in morning mist.

Best Time to Visit November to February (Cooler weather, mystical morning mist over stone pagodas)
Location Mrauk-U Township, Northern Rakhine State
Estimated Cost Archaeological Zone Fee: ~$10 USD / Free for locals

2. Ngapali Beach

History & Background

Ngapali is Myanmar's premier beach resort, celebrated for its 7-kilometer expanse of soft white sand, swaying coconut palms, and crystal-clear turquoise waters along the Bay of Bengal. It retains a peaceful, uncrowded atmosphere with traditional fishing villages nearby.

Best Time to Visit October to April (Sunny days, calm seas, and ideal water temperature)
Location Thandwe Township, Southern Rakhine State
Estimated Cost Free Beach Access / Snorkeling Boat Trip: ~20,000 - 40,000 MMK

3. Sittwe City & Viewpoint Beach

History & Background

Sittwe is the capital of Rakhine State, situated where the Kaladan, Mayu, and Lay Mro rivers enter the Bay of Bengal. Highlights include the vibrant Sittwe Fish Market, the historic Rakhine State Cultural Museum, and the famous 'Point' beach promenade.

Best Time to Visit 4:30 PM - 6:30 PM (Enjoy local sea breeze and spectacular Bay of Bengal sunsets at the Point)
Location Sittwe Township, Coastal Rakhine State
Estimated Cost Free Access / Museum Entry Fee: ~5,000 MMK

4. Mahamuni Pagoda, Kyauktaw

History & Background

The original site of the legendary Mahamuni Buddha Image (which was cast in Rakhine and later moved to Mandalay in 1784). It remains one of the most revered ancient pilgrimage sites in Rakhine State, featuring historic stone relics and shrines.

Best Time to Visit 7:00 AM - 4:00 PM (Quiet morning worship and historical exploration)
Location Kyauktaw Township, Rakhine State (near Siragutta Hill)
Estimated Cost Free Entry / Donations welcome

5. Kanthaya Beach

History & Background

Located south of Ngapali, Kanthaya ('Pleasant Beach') is a quiet, completely untouched coastal escape. Unlike commercialized beaches, it features yellow sand, clear waters, and serene solitude surrounded by local fishing communities.

Best Time to Visit November to March (Ideal for quiet walks, swimming, and nature lovers)
Location Gwa Township, Southern Rakhine State
Estimated Cost Free Beach Access
