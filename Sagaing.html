Top Attraction

1. Sun U Ponnya Shin Pagoda (Sagaing Hill)

History & Background

Founded in 1312 by Ponnya, a central minister of King Thihapate, this pagoda sits atop Nga-pha Hill in Sagaing. According to legend, Ponnya received a magical betel nut box from a hermit, enabling him to build this stupa in a single night.

Best Time to Visit 5:00 AM - 8:00 AM / 4:30 PM - 6:30 PM (Offers panoramic views of Ayeyarwady River)
Location Sagaing Hill, Sagaing Township, Sagaing Region
Estimated Cost Free Entry / Sagaing Hill Camera Fee: ~1,000 MMK

2. Umin Thonze Pagoda (30 Caves Pagoda)

History & Background

Famous for its unique crescent-shaped colonnade housing 45 gilded Buddha statues, Umin Thonze translates to '30 Caves Pagoda'. It was established by Venerable Padugyi Paduma in the 14th century as a meditative retreat for monks.

Best Time to Visit 8:00 AM - 5:00 PM (Great for indoor photography and peaceful meditation)
Location Sagaing Hill Ridge, Sagaing Township, Sagaing Region
Estimated Cost Free Entry / Donations accepted

3. Kaunghmudaw Pagoda (Yaza Mani Cula)

History & Background

Built in 1636 by King Thalun to celebrate the restoration of Ava as the royal capital, this massive hemispherical dome pagoda was modeled after Sri Lanka's Ruwanwelisaya. It houses a sacred tooth relic of Gautama Buddha.

Best Time to Visit 7:00 AM - 6:00 PM (Early morning avoids hot marble tiles)
Location 10 km northern outskirts of Sagaing, Sagaing Region
Estimated Cost Free Entry / Tuk-tuk from Sagaing Center: ~5,000 MMK

4. Thanboddhay Pagoda (Monywa)

History & Background

Rebuilt in 1939 by Thwin Sayadaw, this colorful temple complex resembles Borobudur in Indonesia. It is world-renowned for housing over 500,000 small Buddha images covering every inch of its interior and exterior walls.

Best Time to Visit 8:00 AM - 4:30 PM (Bright daylight enhances the vibrant interior colors)
Location Monywa Township, Sagaing Region (20 km southeast of Monywa)
Estimated Cost Entrance Fee: ~3,000 MMK for foreigners / Free for locals

5. Maha Bodhi Tahtaung & Laykyun Sekkya

History & Background

Founded in 1960 by Bodhi Tahtaung Sayadaw, this spiritual complex features the Laykyun Sekkya standing Buddha (116 meters tall), one of the world's tallest statues, alongside a massive reclining Buddha and a grove of 1,000 sacred Bodhi trees.

Best Time to Visit 7:00 AM - 10:00 AM / 4:00 PM - 6:00 PM (Best light for viewing the tall statue)
Location Khatakan Taung Village, Monywa Township, Sagaing Region
Estimated Cost Free Entrance / Taxi or Motorbike from Monywa: ~10,000 - 15,000 MMK
