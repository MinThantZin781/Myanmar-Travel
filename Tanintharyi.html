Top Attraction

1. Mergui Archipelago (Myeik Islands)

History & Background

Comprising over 800 pristine islands scattered across the Andaman Sea, the Mergui Archipelago is home to the indigenous Moken ethnic group ('Sea Gypsies'). Famous for white-sand beaches, crystal-clear turquoise waters, and untouched coral reefs, it is a world-class diving destination.

Best Time to Visit November to April (Calm seas and optimal sunny weather for island hopping)
Location Andaman Sea, off the coast of Kawthaung and Myeik, Tanintharyi Region
Estimated Cost Day Boat Tour: ~$100 - $150 USD / Multi-day Speedboat Cruise: ~$300+ USD

2. Maungmagan Beach

History & Background

Located just 12 km from Dawei, Maungmagan is one of the oldest beach resorts in Myanmar, popular since the British colonial era. Unlike overdeveloped commercial beaches, it offers a peaceful, authentic coastal atmosphere surrounded by lush green hills.

Best Time to Visit 4:00 PM - 7:00 PM (Ideal for evening walks, fresh seafood, and watching the sunset)
Location Dawei Township, Tanintharyi Region
Estimated Cost Free Beach Access / Fresh Seafood Meals: ~15,000 - 30,000 MMK

3. Grand Father Beach (Po Po Kyauk Beach)

History & Background

Situated on the scenic Dawei Peninsula, Grand Father Beach is renowned for its vast expanse of pure white sand, clear blue waters, and pristine natural beauty. It remains one of the most breathtaking undeveloped beaches in Southeast Asia.

Best Time to Visit 9:00 AM - 4:00 PM (Great for swimming and day trips along the peninsula)
Location Dawei Peninsula, Launglon Township, Tanintharyi Region
Estimated Cost Free Access / Motorbike Rental from Dawei: ~15,000 - 20,000 MMK

4. Kawthaung & Victoria Point

History & Background

Formerly known as Victoria Point during British rule, Kawthaung is the southernmost town of Myanmar, separated from Ranong, Thailand by the Kra Buri River. It features the statue of King Bayinnaung overlooking the sea and serves as the gateway to the Mergui Islands.

Best Time to Visit 8:00 AM - 6:00 PM (Explore Bayinnaung Hill, Maliwan Waterfall, and hot springs)
Location Southernmost Tip, Kawthaung District, Tanintharyi Region
Estimated Cost Free Exploration / Longtail Boat across to Thailand: ~300 THB / 20,000 MMK

5. Myeik Heritage Old Town & Harbor

History & Background

Myeik (Mergui) was a major international trading seaport for centuries, frequented by Portuguese, Dutch, British, and Siamese traders. The town retains historic wooden colonial architecture, bustling seafood markets, bird's nest factories, and pearl farming centers.

Best Time to Visit 7:00 AM - 11:00 AM (Morning fish market) / 4:30 PM - 6:30 PM (Harbor walking)
Location Myeik Waterfront, Myeik Township, Tanintharyi Region
Estimated Cost Free Entry / Local Trishaw/Tuk-tuk Town Tour: ~10,000 - 15,000 MMK
