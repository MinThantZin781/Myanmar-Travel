1. Taukkyan War Cemetery

History & Background

Opened in 1951, this meticulously manicured cemetery is maintained by the Commonwealth War Graves Commission. It serves as the final resting place for over 6,000 Commonwealth soldiers who died fighting in Myanmar during World War II.

Best Time to Visit 8:00 AM - 5:00 PM (Morning or late afternoon)
Location Pyay Road, Taukkyan, Mingaladon Township (North Yangon)
Estimated Cost Free Entrance / Taxi Fare: ~15,000 - 20,000 MMK from Downtown

2. St. Mary's Cathedral

History & Background

Designed by Dutch architect Jos Cuypers and completed in 1899, St. Mary's Cathedral is the largest Catholic church in Myanmar. It survived both a major earthquake in 1930 and Allied bombing during World War II with minimal damage.

Best Time to Visit 8:00 AM - 12:00 PM / 3:00 PM - 5:00 PM
Location Bogyoke Aung San Road, Botahtaung Township, Yangon
Estimated Cost Free Entry (Please dress respectfully for a place of worship)

3. Shwedagon Pagoda

History & Background

Constructed over 2,600 years ago, Shwedagon Pagoda is believed to be the oldest primary stupa in the world. According to legend, two merchant brothers, Tapussa and Bhallika, met the Gautama Buddha and received eight of his sacred hairs, which are enshrined inside this majestic golden pagoda.

Best Time to Visit 5:00 AM - 8:00 AM / 4:30 PM - 8:00 PM (Best for Sunset)
Location Arzarnee Road, Dagon Township, Yangon
Estimated Cost Entrance Fee: 20,000 MMK (~$10 USD for foreigners) / Free for locals

4. Sule Pagoda

History & Background

Over 2,500 years old, Sule Pagoda predates Shwedagon itself and is named after the Sule Nat (spirit) who guided the discovery of Shwedagon's hill site. During British colonial rule, the city of Yangon was redesigned with Sule Pagoda as its exact central roundabout point.

Best Time to Visit 6:00 AM - 8:00 PM (Evening time offers pleasant weather)
Location Junction of Sule Pagoda Road and Maha Bandula Road, Downtown Yangon
Estimated Cost Entrance Fee: 10,000 MMK / Taxi Fare: 4,000 - 6,000 MMK from Downtown

5. Bogyoke Aung San Market

History & Background

Built in 1926 during British rule, it was originally named Scott Market after the Municipal Commissioner. After Myanmar gained independence in 1948, it was renamed in honor of General Aung San. It is famous for its cobblestone streets and intact colonial architecture.

Best Time to Visit 10:00 AM - 4:00 PM (Closed on Mondays and Gazette Holidays)
Location Bogyoke Aung San Road, Pabedan Township, Yangon
Estimated Cost Free Entry / Shopping & Snacks: 15,000 - 50,000 MMK
